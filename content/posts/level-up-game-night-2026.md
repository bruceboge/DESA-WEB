The Dedan Kimathi University of Technology Engineering Students Association (DESA) welcomed student engineers to the **Level Up Game Night** at the School of Engineering complex.

Designed as an evening to unwind, connect across cohorts, and build camaraderie beyond lecture halls and CAD laboratories, students brought their A-game for high-energy board and party games.

### Event Highlights & Activities
- **Team Challenges**: Charades, Poker, Mafia, Go Fish, and Kenya@50.
- **Board & Card Battles**: Chess, Checkers, UNO, Uno Reverse, and deck strategy games.
- **Venue**: Room A3, School of Engineering (SOE)
- **Time**: 4:00 PM – 7:00 PM

### Cohort Community & Connection
Engineering school requires rigorous problem-solving, and events like Game Night reinforce the strong supportive network within DESA. Students across 1st to 5th year came together for fun, networking, and team spirit.
