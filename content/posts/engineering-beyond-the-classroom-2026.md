The Dedan Kimathi University Engineering Students Association (DESA) in collaboration with the **IEEE Women in Engineering (WIE) DeKUT Affinity Group** hosted an empowering student development session titled **"Engineering, Beyond The Classroom"**.

Held at Room A2 in the School of Engineering (SOE) on Wednesday, 23rd September from 17:00hrs, the interactive session unpacked what it takes to excel as a holistic engineering professional in today's evolving technical landscape.

### Core Session Pillars

#### 01: Opportunities Beyond The Classroom
- Practical avenues for securing engineering attachments, industrial internships, and corporate apprenticeships.
- Competing in national and international engineering hackathons, design challenges, and student symposiums.
- Building active industry exposure while still on campus.

#### 02: The Engineering Toolkit
- Gaining hands-on technical skills, industry-standard simulation software, prototyping tools, and practical problem-solving.
- Translating academic theory into deployable hardware and software solutions.

#### 03: The Engineer Behind The CV
- Holistic personal growth, building self-confidence, effective presentation and technical communication.
- Cultivating team dynamics, leadership qualities, and maintaining personal mental wellbeing under rigorous academic loads.

### Special Focus: Suicide Prevention Month & Mental Health
September marks **Suicide Prevention Month**, and the session prioritized engineer wellbeing and supportive peer networks:
> *"You are not alone. Your wellbeing matters. Let's support each other, speak up, and be kind."*

Engineering is demanding, and DESA and IEEE WIE are committed to ensuring our community provides a safe, encouraging environment where students lift each other up.
